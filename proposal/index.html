One selected plan.
Five clear payments.

Each payment is due at the beginning of its phase, following review and approval of the preceding phase’s agreed deliverables. Phase 1 begins after the engagement is agreed and the kickoff payment is received.

Only the schedule for your selected plan applies. These allocations sum to that plan’s first-year development fee. The proposed annual renewal is shown separately below.

Separate operating costs

Software delivery ≠ provider usage.

The development fee covers the agreed build and integration work. The following are excluded unless expressly agreed in writing:

  • Hosting, domains, storage, backups, email and monitoring subscriptions.
  • Video licences, streaming, recording storage and webinar capacity.
  • Charting licences, market-data subscriptions and exchange permissions.
  • AI subscriptions, model usage, token charges and related processing.
  • Payment provider charges, transaction fees and merchant onboarding costs.
  • Other paid plugins, third-party subscriptions and externally commissioned content.
